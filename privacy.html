Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

At BrainStorm Games, the creators of the free-to-play educational game ImmunoBlitz, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we do and do not collect, how it is used, and the steps we take to protect your data when you visit our website or play our games across web and mobile platforms.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We are committed to providing a safe educational environment. We do not actively collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number simply by you visiting our site or playing the game. The only exception is if you voluntarily choose to contact us directly via our support email.

2. Automatically Collected Data and Analytics

To understand how players interact with our site and to improve the educational experience, we use Google Analytics. This service automatically collects basic, non-personally identifiable log data, which may include:

  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages viewed and time spent on the site
  • General geographic location (at the city or country level)
  • Referring URLs

This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.

3. Third-Party Advertising and Cookies

To keep ImmunoBlitz free for educators, students, and families, we display advertisements using third-party vendors, including Google AdSense (for web) and Google AdMob (for Android).

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • These services may use an advertising ID to personalize the ads you see.

Opting Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Children's Privacy

ImmunoBlitz is an educational game designed to be safe and appropriate for learners of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Furthermore, we make every effort to configure our third-party advertising networks (such as AdSense and AdMob) to only display family-friendly, universally appropriate advertisements.

5. External Links

Our website may contain links to external educational resources or partner sites. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every site you visit, as we have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or regulatory requirements. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact us directly at info@brainstorm-games.com.
