Educational & Medical Disclaimer

Important information about the purpose and limitations of ImmunoBlitz and the BrainStorm Games Learning Hub.

Last Updated: 8 October 2026

ImmunoBlitz and the educational material on this website are designed for science education and entertainment. They help learners explore concepts related to pathogens, immunity, infection, vaccination, antibiotics and public health, but they do not reproduce the full complexity of medicine or human biology.

Not medical advice

Nothing in the game, Learning Hub or website should be used to diagnose a condition, choose a treatment, decide whether to take or stop medication, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

1. Educational Purpose

The game uses simplified rules and visual representations to make biological relationships easier to explore. Health bars, waves, upgrade points, visible pathogen objects and instant game effects are teaching and gameplay devices rather than literal representations of physiology.

The Learning Hub provides more detailed explanations and links to authoritative sources so that players can compare the game model with real science.

2. Scientific Simplification

Human immunology is complex. Real immune responses involve many cell types, tissues, signalling molecules, genes and environmental factors. The course and timing of infection can differ substantially between people and between diseases.

ImmunoBlitz intentionally leaves out many of those details. A simplified model can be useful for learning, but it should not be interpreted as a complete medical or biological simulation.

3. Scientific Accuracy and Updates

BrainStorm Games aims to present educational content accurately and to use authoritative sources where appropriate. However, scientific knowledge, public-health guidance and medical practice can change over time, and no educational website can guarantee that every statement remains complete or current indefinitely.

Readers who need current professional guidance should consult appropriate healthcare services or official public-health authorities.

4. Vaccines, Medicines and Other Interventions

References to vaccines, antibiotics, antiseptics, masks or other interventions are included to teach broad scientific and public-health concepts. Their appearance in the game does not constitute a recommendation for any individual person.

Real decisions about medicines and vaccination depend on factors that a videogame cannot assess, including diagnosis, medical history, age, dosage, contraindications and professional guidance.

5. No Doctor–Patient Relationship

Using the website, playing ImmunoBlitz or contacting BrainStorm Games does not create a doctor–patient, therapist–patient or other clinical professional relationship.

6. External Sources and Links

The Learning Hub may link to external organisations such as health agencies, research repositories or educational resources. External links are provided for further reading. BrainStorm Games does not control third-party websites and cannot guarantee their continued availability, content or privacy practices.

7. Classroom Use

Teachers may use ImmunoBlitz as a learning resource, but the game is best used alongside appropriate instruction. Educators are encouraged to discuss where the game's mechanics simplify biology and to use the Learning Hub or other trusted science resources for deeper explanation.

8. Emergencies and Personal Health Concerns

If you have a medical emergency or urgent health concern, do not rely on this website or contact BrainStorm Games for assistance. Seek appropriate professional or emergency medical help in your location.

9. Limitation of Reliance

Use of the website and game is at the user's discretion. To the extent permitted by law, BrainStorm Games is not responsible for decisions made by relying on educational game mechanics or website content as if they were individual medical advice.

10. Changes to This Disclaimer

This disclaimer may be updated as ImmunoBlitz, the Learning Hub or relevant services change. The revision date shown above indicates the latest published version.

The intended learning cycle

Play → notice → question → investigate. Use ImmunoBlitz to generate curiosity, then use reliable scientific information to understand the real biology behind the game.
