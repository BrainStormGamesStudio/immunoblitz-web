Get in Touch with BrainStorm Games

Whether you are a student, a teacher looking to incorporate interactive science into your classroom, or simply a fan of ImmunoBlitz, we would love to hear from you. Your feedback is what allows us to keep improving our educational games.

How Can We Help You?

To help us direct your inquiry to the right place, please let us know the nature of your message when you email us. We frequently assist with:

  • Educator Support: Are you using ImmunoBlitz in your biology or science curriculum? Let us know if you need specific features, lesson plan integrations, or have suggestions for future educational topics.
  • Technical Support & Bug Reports: If you encountered an issue while playing the game on either your web browser or an Android device, please include what device you are using and a brief description of the problem.
  • General Feedback: We love hearing what you enjoy about the game and what you think could make the experience even better.
  • Business & Press Inquiries: For partnership opportunities, interviews, or media requests regarding our independent micro-studio.
