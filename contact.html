If You Are Reporting a Technical Problem

Including a few details in your first message can help us understand the issue more quickly:

  • the device you were using;
  • whether you were playing in a web browser or on Android;
  • the browser and operating system, if relevant;
  • what you were doing immediately before the problem occurred;
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
  • a screenshot of an error message, if one appeared.

For Teachers and Schools

If you are contacting us about classroom use, it is helpful to mention the approximate age group, science topic and how you are planning to use the game. You can also explore the Teacher's Guide to Using ImmunoBlitz and the wider Learning Hub before getting in touch.

Scientific and Medical Questions

Please do not send personal medical information

BrainStorm Games provides educational content and cannot provide diagnosis, treatment recommendations or individual medical advice. If your question concerns your own health or symptoms, please contact an appropriate healthcare professional instead.

Privacy When You Contact Us

If you email us, we will receive the information you choose to include in your message and the email address from which you contact us. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information. For more detail, see our Privacy Policy.

Studio Location

BrainStorm Games is based in Malta. We do not currently operate a public walk-in office, so email is the appropriate contact method.

Response Times

We aim to reply to messages within approximately 2–3 business days, although responses may take longer during busy periods or when an enquiry requires technical investigation.
