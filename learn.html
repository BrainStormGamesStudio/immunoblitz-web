The ImmunoBlitz Learning Hub

Go beyond the game and explore the biology behind infection, immunity and disease prevention. These free guides are designed for students, educators, families and curious players who want to understand both the real science and the way ImmunoBlitz turns that science into an interactive model.

Learn the Science Behind the Strategy

Start with pathogens and the body's natural barriers, then move into immune cells, inflammation, vaccination, antimicrobial resistance and disease transmission. The final guides focus on how ImmunoBlitz works as a scientific model and how teachers can use the game in class.

Recommended Learning Path

1. Meet the threat

Start with viruses, bacteria and fungi, then explore how infections spread.

2. Stop entry

Study skin, mucus and other first-line barriers.

3. Fight what gets through

Learn about macrophages, neutrophils, lymphocytes and inflammation.

4. Prepare for the future

Explore vaccination, immune memory and antimicrobial resistance.

5. Critique the model

Finish by comparing ImmunoBlitz with real biology and using the teacher activities.

For Teachers

The Learning Hub can support lessons on microorganisms, infection, immunity, vaccination, antibiotics, public health and scientific modelling. Each article is designed to stand alone, while the Teacher's Guide provides ideas for turning gameplay into structured classroom learning.
